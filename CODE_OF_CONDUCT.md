# Code of Conduct / 行为准则

## English

ICUAC contributors and community members must communicate respectfully, focus on technical facts, welcome constructive feedback, and avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, spam, or deliberate disruption.

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove content and restrict participation when behavior harms the project or community. Scope includes Issues, Pull Requests, Discussions, release comments, and other spaces officially associated with ICUAC.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's security/contact facilities. Good-faith reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.

## 中文

ICUAC 贡献者与社区成员必须保持尊重、聚焦技术事实、接受建设性意见，并禁止骚扰、歧视、威胁、人肉、垃圾信息或故意破坏。

当行为损害项目或社区时，维护者可编辑、隐藏或删除内容并限制参与。范围包括 Issues、Pull Requests、Discussions、Release 评论及其他 ICUAC 官方空间。

行为问题请通过仓库安全/联系渠道私密报告，善意报告会在合理范围内保密处理。
